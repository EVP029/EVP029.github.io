Mecha-Paws

A 2D wave-based game for PC, inspired by the animation of the 1930s with Disney and Fleischer as the main references. Built in one semester (August 2024 to January 2025) and presented at the university fair.

I led the art: concept art of the protagonist, Paquito, the renders and all 8 animations, made in Unity and Procreate.

  • Style1930s rubber-hose animation
  • PlatformPC
