Enfys: Trials of Twilight

A 2D pixel-art platformer built in Unity as the final project for Introduction to Videogames. The player crosses an autumn forest of broken platforms and reaches a ruined hall, with dynamic lighting that shifts from a misty dawn to a warm sunset.

I made the whole project on my own: game design, programming, level design, lighting and the integration of all the art.
