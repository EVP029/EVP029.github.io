About me

Emilio Velázquez Puente

Hi! I'm Emilio Velázquez (E V P), an artist and animator in training. I study Game Development and Animation Engineering at Universidad Panamericana in Mexico City.

I have loved drawing since I was a child, and I have kept growing through self-learning, the guidance of dedicated mentors and the constant support of my parents. I work in traditional media, drawing and painting with acrylic, oil and watercolor, and in digital art and animation with Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Procreate. I also bring my work into the engine, rigging and animating characters in Unity.

I enjoy bringing ideas to life through visual storytelling and collaborating on projects that challenge creativity and innovation. Over the years I have worked on artistic projects of different scales, developed leadership skills as art lead, and learned the value of being part of effective, goal-driven teams.
